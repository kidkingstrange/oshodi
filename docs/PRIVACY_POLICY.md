# Privacy Policy — Oshodimushin: Lagos Street RPG

**Last Updated:** November 3, 2026  
**Developer / Publisher:** Danfo Studios  
**Application:** Oshodimushin: Lagos Street RPG (Package: `com.danfostudios.oshodimushin`)

---

## 1. Introduction & Overview

Danfo Studios ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting player privacy. **Oshodimushin: Lagos Street RPG** is designed from the ground up as a single-player, **offline-first** life simulation roleplaying game. 

This Privacy Policy explains what information is (and is not) collected, how third-party services operate within the app, and how your privacy is protected.

By installing and playing Oshodimushin: Lagos Street RPG, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe your gameplay belongs to you. **Danfo Studios does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII).** Specifically, we do not:
- Collect your real name, physical address, email, or telephone number.
- Access your device contacts, microphone, camera, or SMS messages.
- Request, track, or access precise GPS location data.
- Collect biometric, health, or financial account credentials.
- Create user accounts or require online registration to play.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage (Offline-First)

All your gameplay progression, character stats, inventory items, quest achievements, audio settings, and local save files are stored **strictly on your local device** within the Android application sandbox (`user://`). 

This data is never uploaded to external servers or synced with remote databases. You retain total control over your game data:
- You can reset or delete your local save at any time via the in-game Settings menu.
- Clearing the application cache/data in Android system settings or uninstalling the application permanently removes all local data from your device.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

To provide optional rewarded video ads and secure in-app supporter purchases, the app integrates standard Google Play developer libraries. These libraries operate under Google's Privacy Policy:

### A. Google Play In-App Billing (Google LLC)
- **Purpose:** Enables players to purchase non-consumable supporter perks (such as "Remove Ads") and optional cosmetic packs.
- **Data Handled:** Purchase tokens, order IDs, and entitlement validation.
- **Financial Privacy:** All financial transactions and payment details (such as credit cards, debit cards, or mobile money) are processed directly and securely by Google Play. Danfo Studios never receives, sees, or stores your financial or banking information.
- For more information, visit: [Google Play Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### B. Google AdMob (Google LLC)
- **Purpose:** Provides optional, player-initiated rewarded ads (such as watching an ad to regain game energy). There are **zero forced pop-up ads, zero banner ads, and zero interstitial ads** during gameplay.
- **Data Collected by AdMob:** Google AdMob may automatically collect certain device data, such as device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID), general coarse IP location, device model, operating system version, and diagnostic/performance metrics for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and frequency capping.
- **Ad Choices & Opt-Out:** You can manage or reset your advertising ID and opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your Android device settings (**Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**).
- For details on how Google processes ad data, visit: [Google Advertising Privacy FAQ](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Oshodimushin: Lagos Street RPG is an all-ages urban roleplaying adventure intended for a general audience (recommended for players aged 13 and older). 

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If a parent or guardian believes that personal information has been inadvertently transmitted through third-party ad services, please contact us immediately, and we will take necessary steps to coordinate with the ad provider to address it.

---

## 6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because we do not maintain remote user databases or collect personal accounts, we do not store player data on remote servers.

- **To delete your gameplay data:** Open the in-game Settings screen and tap "Delete Save", or navigate to your device **Settings > Apps > Oshodimushin RPG > Storage > Clear Data**.
- **Entitlements:** If you purchase the "Remove Ads" item, your ownership is tied to your Google Play account. You can reinstall the game on any compatible Android device logged into that same Google account and tap "Restore Previous Purchases" in the game's Special Store to recover your purchase.

---

## 7. Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect our application code and assets against tampering. Local save files and configuration files are stored securely inside the private Android app sandbox directory, inaccessible to other standard apps without root access.
