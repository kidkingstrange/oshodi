# 05 — Research Guide (for the agent's web browsing)

## Purpose
Make the game feel authentic to Oshodi–Isolo without copying anyone's work. Research feeds **original** writing and design.

## What to research (save each as a note in `docs/research/`)
1. **Geography:** districts, major roads, landmarks, transport nodes in Oshodi, Isolo, Ejigbo, Mafoluku, Ilasamaja, Okota, Ajao Estate, Shogunle. → `geography.md`
2. **Transport life:** danfo, keke, okada, BRT, bus parks, park dues, typical routes and pain points. → `transport.md`
3. **Markets and trade:** what is sold, how haggling works, typical price ranges (note the date of each price). → `markets.md`
4. **Work and jobs:** what each class and subclass actually does day to day, tools, risks, slang for each. → `jobs.md`
5. **Language:** Nigerian Pidgin and Lagos English phrases, greetings, banter, with meanings and tone. → `language.md`
6. **Daily-life pressures:** power supply, generators, fuel, rent, flooding, traffic, safety. → `daily_life.md`
7. **Regulation context:** how traffic enforcement, park unions and street trade are commonly described in reputable news (for realistic Situations, not as accusations). → `regulation.md`
8. **Humour and culture:** typical situations people laugh about, food, music genres (describe, never reproduce lyrics), festivals. → `culture.md`

## Source rules
- Prefer: reputable Nigerian and international news outlets, government and transport authority pages, academic and NGO reports, and well-reviewed local-knowledge sources.
- Use at least **two independent sources** for any factual claim you put in the game (places, prices, rules). Mark uncertain items as `UNVERIFIED`.
- Treat forums and social media as colour only, never as fact.
- Note each source (title, site, date visited) in the research note.

## Copyright and originality rules
- **Never copy text, images, music, maps or data** from any site into the game. Paraphrase fully, in your own words, and build original characters, jokes and scenarios.
- Do not reproduce song lyrics, poems, jokes, or film/TV material, and do not recreate real comedians' or skit-makers' content.
- Do not download images from the web into the game. Create original art or use assets whose licence allows commercial use, and log them in `ASSET_LICENSES.md`.
- Map data: use only as inspiration; draw the in-game map yourself.

## People and sensitivity rules
- No real people, businesses, or brands as characters. Use fictional names.
- Do not present allegations of corruption or crime as fact about any real organisation. Keep Situations generic and fictional.
- Portray groups (ethnic, religious, professional) fairly. Humour targets situations and human foibles, not identities.
- Do not include any private or personal information about individuals.

## Note template
```
# <Topic>
Date researched: 
Summary (own words):
Key facts (each with sources):
Ideas for Situations/NPCs/items (original):
UNVERIFIED items:
Sources:
```

## How research is used
Before writing any Situation, NPC, item or location in a phase, read the relevant note. After writing, add a line in `docs/research/USAGE.md` listing which notes inspired which content ids. *Why: traceability, and proof that content is original.*

## Limits
If a site blocks access, move on to another source; do not try to bypass blocks or paywalls.
