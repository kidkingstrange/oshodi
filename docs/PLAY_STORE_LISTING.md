## 2. Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

Welcome to Oshodi! Step into the bustling, high-stakes, vibrant streets of Lagos, Nigeria. In Oshodimushin: Lagos Street RPG, you experience the energy, humor, unpredictability, and resilience of the daily hustle.

From the chaotic underbridge trade counters to the high-speed Danfo bus lanes of the Oshodi-Isolo Expressway, your survival depends on quick thinking, street smarts, and managing your hard-earned Naira.

Will you build a commercial empire, command the roads, mediate community disputes, or climb from a street boy to an influential community leader? Every choice shapes your story, your Respect, and your Heat on the streets.

CHOOSE FROM 6 UNIQUE HUSTLE CLASSES:
• Trader: Master the market stalls. Buy low, haggle hard, outwit counterfeiters, and stock the goods people need. Specialize as a Boutique Owner, Electronics Mogul, Provision Wholesaler, Raw Foodstuff Dealer, Spare Parts Tycoon, or Curio Exporter.
• Transporter: Command the iconic yellow Danfo buses or nimble Keke tricycles. Manage fuel, dodge traffic gridlocks, keep rowdy passengers calm, and keep your bus running. Specialize as a Danfo Driver, Keke Cruiser, Okada Rider, Interstate Pilot, Bus Fleet Owner, or Luxury Hauler.
• Area Boy: Navigate street politics with respect, wit, and charisma. Secure parking zones, mediate rivalries, and protect your territory without crossing the law. Specialize as a Street Mediator, Zone Organizer, Agbero Tactician, Park Overseer, Youth Leader, or Cultural Organizer.
• Working Class: Balance long commutes on third-party buses with office politics and side hustles. Juggle overtime stamina and career promotions while building your family savings. Specialize as a Bank Clerk, Civil Servant, IT Tech Support, School Teacher, Factory Supervisor, or Corporate Hustler.
• Road Official: Don the uniform and take charge of high-traffic intersections. Manage traffic flows, inspect vehicle papers, deal with stubborn motorists, and balance your official duty with compassion. Specialize as a Traffic Warden, Highway Inspector, Toll Marshal, Licensing Clerk, Rapid Responder, or Chief Superintendent.
• Faith Leader: Guide your community through neighborhood counselling, morning devotions, and community charity. Calm heated land disputes and inspire people with wisdom. Specialize as an Evangelist, Imam, Tenement Pastor, Mission Organizer, Community Deacon, or Traditional Elder.

DEEP ROLEPLAYING & AUTHENTIC CHOICES:
• Over 60 authentic, hand-crafted Lagos Street Situations: Resolve market fire scares, unexpected police checkpoints, sudden flash floods, fuel scarcity queues, and neighborhood disputes.
• Dynamic Respect & Heat mechanics: High Respect unlocks influential allies and cheaper market rates; high Heat attracts road officials, agbero levies, and vigilant inspectors.
• 100% Offline-First Gameplay: Zero internet connection required to enjoy the full game. Perfect for commuting, battery-saver mode, and areas with spotty data reception.
• No Pay-to-Win: All story content, classes, subclasses, and milestones are unlockable purely through smart gameplay. Supporter upgrades and quick rewarded energy boosts are entirely optional.
• Authentic Danfo Poster Art Direction: Clean typography, high-contrast urban visual style, and evocative soundscapes celebrating everyday Lagosian tenacity.

Can you survive the morning rush, out-hustle the street challenges, and write your name into Oshodi folklore?

Download Oshodimushin: Lagos Street RPG today and start your hustle!
