# Research Note: Geography of Oshodi Axis
Date researched: 2026-10-30
Summary (own words):
Oshodi is one of the most prominent transportation and commercial nerve centres in Lagos State. It sits strategically at the intersection connecting Lagos Mainland, the Murtala Muhammed International Airport (via Ajao Estate / Mafoluku), the Lagos–Abeokuta Expressway, and the Apapa–Oshodi Expressway leading toward the ports.

Key facts:
- Oshodi Underbridge: Historically known as a massive chaotic nexus of buses, transformed into a managed transport corridor while still maintaining an intense pedestrian and street trading energy.
- Oshodi Interchange: A multi-terminal modern bus interchange constructed to consolidate intra-city BRT and interstate transit.
- Oshodi Market: Sprawling retail and wholesale trading hub famous for textiles, electronics, food items, and roadside hawking.
- Bolade Junction: Key landmark intersection providing access into inner Oshodi communities, train tracks, and residential quarters.
