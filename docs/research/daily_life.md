# Research Note: Daily Life, Food & Economy in Oshodi
Date researched: 2026-10-30
Summary (own words):
Daily life for ordinary Lagos residents revolves around balancing energy and daily expenses against cash flow. Key daily costs include street food (breakfast and dinner from local canteens or mama-put stalls), transport to and from work, electricity generator fueling ("I better pass my neighbour" or heavy diesel gensets during power cuts), and weekly/monthly room rent.

Key facts:
- Standard meal at a roadside canteen (e.g. rice with stew and meat or amala with ewedu/gbegiri) ranges between ₦1,000 – ₦2,000.
- Power cuts ("light off") frequently occur in the afternoon and evening blocks, prompting businesses to switch to generator power.
- Street news spreads through word-of-mouth, bus stop banter, newspaper stands, and social media chatter.
