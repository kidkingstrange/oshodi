# Research Note: Jobs & Livelihoods in Oshodi
Date researched: 2026-10-31
Summary (own words):
Daily livelihoods across the Oshodi-Isolo corridor rely on street-level ingenuity, micro-retail, informal transport, food services, and logistics. Traders specialize from perishable greens and foodstuff to durable consumer electronics, phone accessories, and textile fabrics. Commercial drivers (danfo, keke, dispatch) navigate heavy traffic density, strict municipal corridor regulations, fuel supply fluctuations, and union ticketing structures.

Key facts (each with sources):
- Street markets in Oshodi host thousands of micro-retail stalls with tight associations (National Association of Nigerian Traders / market associations).
- Mama Put canteens serve heavy caloric staples (amala, pounded yam, jollof, egusi) fueling drivers, market porters, and office workers from 6:00 AM to midnight.
- POS / Mobile Money agents operate kiosks at transit hubs providing cash withdrawals and bill payments where bank ATMs are sparse or crowded.
- Commercial dispatch riders operate two-wheel deliveries linking Lagos mainland markets with retail customers across the metropolitan area.

- Chidi: Ladipo-connected electronics merchant offering phone cords, chargers, and generator spark plugs.
