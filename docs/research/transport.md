# Research Note: Transport Life & Traffic in Oshodi
Date researched: 2026-10-30
Summary (own words):
Commercial passenger movement in Oshodi is dominated by commercial yellow buses (danfo), three-wheeled tricycles (keke), dispatch motorcycles, and BRT corridor buses. Drivers and conductors operate in high-pressure rush-hour rhythms, balancing vehicle fuel, union ticket dues, park loading turns, and passenger fares.

Key facts:
- Peak rush hours occur between 6:30 AM – 9:30 AM (morning rush toward commercial hubs) and 4:30 PM – 8:30 PM (evening return traffic).
- Fares fluctuate dynamically based on rainfall, fuel queues, and traffic congestion along the expressways.
- Conductors call out destinations rapidly using recognizable abbreviations and rhythmic chants.
- Inter-location short hops within the local district typically cost between ₦200 to ₦400.

Ideas for Situations/NPCs/items (original):
- Travel system connecting the four local stops with 1 time block travel and small fare costs.
- Situations involving conductor change shortages, fuel station queues, and traffic bottlenecks.

UNVERIFIED items:
- Daily union levy changes during public holiday schedules.
